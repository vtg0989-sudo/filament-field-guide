How fees are figured: Etsy's US fees as of mid-2026: $0.20 to list, a 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping, and payment processing of 3% plus $0.25. Offsite Ads, if a sale comes from one, adds 15% for shops under $10,000 a year. Check Etsy's fee page for your country.

Cutting costs: filament is usually the biggest line. Buying multi-spool packs or using a discount code lowers your cost per gram. Polymaker filament with code VINCENTGALLARDO supports this site.
